# Artwork provenance

The botanical visual reference is `preview.png` from
[rohan-patnaik/Solitaire](https://github.com/rohan-patnaik/Solitaire), revision
`f3ed72e235af91920daa92bee5a3453f93cd9030`.

The reference uses orange, red poppies and tulips, olive leaves and stems,
navy details, golden suns, cream flowers, and navy/cream botanical card backs.
Solitare adapts this direction as a single composed folk-botanical frame: trailing
branches at the top corners, a central sun flanked by cream blossoms, large
poppies and navy bells at the bottom corners, and a tall central plant with
connected sweeping vines. The playing area remains open. The table base follows
the current palette; configurable `floral_*` colors retain the reference hues
with a 22% accent tint. Card faces use warm off-white paper, large botanical
illustrations, and readable conventional rank and suit marks at both ends.
Card backs use dense interlocking cream foliage and red flower medallions on navy.

All displayed florals are original QPainter vector paths in
`src/ui/card_art.cpp` and `src/ui/floral_art.cpp`; no upstream raster artwork is
bundled or traced.
The upstream MIT notice is retained in `UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt` for attribution.
The five game modes and selectable rules also follow that project's collection.
